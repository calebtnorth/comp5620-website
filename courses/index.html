Courses

COMP 5620
Theory and practice of designing interfaces for interactive systems, usability engineering techniques; implementing and evaluating interfaces. The objectives of this course are: (i) to introduce students to the interdisciplinary theoretical foundations of the field of User Interface Design; (ii) to illustrate practical approaches to translating the theory into building and evaluating interactive systems; (iii) to expose students to the tasks of researching, critically analyzing and reporting on an UID topic; and (iv) to provide hands-on experience in designing or modifying and experimentally evaluating an interactive system.
COMP 7620
Theory and practice of human computer interaction; designing, implementing and evaluating human computer interation. The objectives of this course are: (i) to introduce students to the interdisciplinary theoretical foundations of the field of Human-Computer Interaction; (ii) to illustrate practical approaches to translating the theory into building and evaluating interactive systems; (iii) to expose students to the tasks of researching, critically analyzing and reporting on an HCI topic; and (iv) to provide hands-on experience in designing or modifying and experimentally evaluating an interactive system.
